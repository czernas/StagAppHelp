## Your study at a glance

At the top of the **Study** tab you see your programme and year, **Credits** (how many you have out of those enrolled, and how many are acknowledged), your **Average**, and the number of **Exams** and **Pre-exams** passed. With **Year**, switch between academic years or show the **Full study**.

## Progress

**Progress** lists your subjects and grades by semester. Tap a grade to see which ratings the subject allows. Acknowledged subjects are marked **Acknowledged**. If you have a thesis assigned, you'll find your **Thesis** here too.

## Counting the average

Change how the average is calculated in **Settings** › **Grades & timetable** › **Counting of the average**: **Weighted** or **Failure at beginning**.

## Several studies

Switch between studies in **Account**, or by touching and holding your initials at the top right (see **Several studies and accounts**).

### In the classic look

Your grades are in your profile (**Study** tab › **Show profile**, or in the **Menu**):

- The header shows credits, average, exams, pre-exams and year. The year button switches the academic year or shows the **Full study**.
- The **Study** tab lists subjects and grades by semester, and the **Progress** tab shows completed credits, subjects by semester and your thesis.
- Set **Counting of the average** in the profile's last tab, **Settings**.
