## Room number is too long

In some cases, a room may have a numeric designation that is too long to be displayed in full for a timetable activity. In this case, you can find out the room number from the timetable activity detail (by tapping on the activity).

## The number is not shown in the details either

This condition may be caused by poor data synchronization. For example, it was not possible to download all the information from STAG when updating the database, or the data was stored incorrectly in the database.

To fix this problem, you can force a data update in the **Settings** tab in **Menu**.

## Previous solutions don't help?

Try uninstalling and reinstalling the application.
