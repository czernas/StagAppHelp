## Your friends

In **Friends**, **Shared with me** lists everyone who shares their timetable with you. Tap a friend to open their page and timetable.

## Favourite friends on Today

Touch and hold a friend and choose **Add to favorites**, or on their page choose **Pin to Today** from the **⋯** menu. You'll then see your favourite friends, and where they are right now, on the **Today** tab under **Friends**.

Remove a friend with **Remove from friends**.

### In the classic look

**Menu** › **Friends** lists all your friends with their current and next class. Add them to your favourites with the heart, and you'll find their cards on the **Favorites** tab.
