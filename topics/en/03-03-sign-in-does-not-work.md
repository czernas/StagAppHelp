## The most common causes

Most often, sign-in is blocked by a limit on the IS/STAG server. This happens for example:

- **After a wrong password.** After repeated wrong passwords, STAG may block sign-in for several tens of minutes.
- **After a wrong username.** Use your username for the web version of IS/STAG, not your e-mail.
- **After a recent password change.** The new password may not have reached STAG yet. Try your previous password.
- **In your first year, before the semester starts.** Your account may not be enabled for signing in from the app yet.

### Signing in on your university's website?

On the sign-in page, use the section for your **school credentials**, not the _IS/STAG user database_.

![](webview_login.jpeg)

### Studying at TUL?

Use the **Jednotné přihlášení TUL** (TUL single sign-on) section.

![](webview_login_tul.png)

### Studying at OSU?

You often need the initial password you were given when you started your studies. If that doesn't help either, contact your university's network administrator about signing in through the API.

## Nothing helps?

Write to us through **Feedback** in **Account** (in the classic look, in the **Menu**) and choose the sign-in topic. Include your username, university and an e-mail address we can reply to.
