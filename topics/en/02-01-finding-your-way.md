## What's in the app

The app has what you need most during your studies: your timetable, grades, exam sign-up including the waiting queue, study progress, search, free rooms, the canteen, the academic calendar, and news and offers from your university. You can also share timetables with your friends.

You'll find more at [univerzita.app](https://www.univerzita.app).

## The new look (University Plus)

The app has four tabs at the bottom:

- **Today**: what's on now, what's left today and what not to forget.
- **Timetable**: your whole timetable by day, by week, as a list or as a grid.
- **Study**: grades, study progress and exam dates.
- **Campus**: search, friends, rooms, the canteen, the academic calendar, offers and news.

Your initials at the top right open **Account**: sign-in and studies, notices from your university, hidden subjects, settings, Help, feedback and information about the app. Touch and hold your initials to switch to another study quickly.

### In the classic look

The tab bar has **Study**, **Timetable**, **Explore**, **Favorites** and **Menu**. Open your profile with grades and exam dates from the **Study** tab with **Show profile**, or at the top of the **Menu**. The **Menu** also holds rooms, search, the schedule, friends, offers, the canteen, Help and settings. On iPad, a sidebar replaces the tab bar.

## Schools without IS/STAG

If your school doesn't use IS/STAG, the app shows only offers and news from your university. The timetable, grades, rooms, friends and the academic calendar need IS/STAG. ČVUT has its own screens in the app.

## The welcome tour

On first launch, the app walks you through choosing your university, signing in and setting up notifications. You can change notifications at any time in the settings (see **Notifications**).
