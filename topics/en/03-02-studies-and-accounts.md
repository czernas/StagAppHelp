## Several studies

If you have more than one study (for example a bachelor's and a master's, or two programmes), the app finds all of them when you sign in. The list is refreshed from STAG regularly, so a new study appears and a finished one is marked **Finished**.

## Switching studies

Open **Account** behind your initials at the top right and tap the study you want to see. To switch faster, touch and hold your initials.

## Another account

You can also sign in to another account, for example at another university. In **Account**, open **Accounts** and tap **Add account**.

## Refreshing studies and signing out

Touch and hold a study in **Account** and choose **Refresh studies** or **Sign out…**. If you have more than one account, switch to another one first: the current account can't be signed out.

### In the classic look

Switch studies and accounts in your profile with the button at the top right. That's also where you'll find **Sign in to another account**, **Refresh studies** for each account and the sign-out button.
