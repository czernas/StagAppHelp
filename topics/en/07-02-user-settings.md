## Where can I find the user settings?

A button with the application logo can be found in the middle of the bottom bar in the application. When you tap on it, you will be taken to the profile where you can find the user's settings.

## What can I change here?

In this setting you can:

- Change the way the **average** is calculated
- Change the **e-mail** for feedback for the job offers
- Toggle to display only **relevant job offers**
