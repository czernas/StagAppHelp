## Which notifications the app sends

In **Settings** › **Notifications**, turn on:

- **New rating**: a new grade or pre-exam.
- **New exam date**: a newly published exam date.
- **News from university**.
- **Before start of timetable action**: a reminder 5, 10 or 15 minutes before a class. **Show with highest priority** delivers them as time-sensitive, so they get through a Focus if you allow it there.
- **On app turned off**: a reminder that a closed app can't refresh data in the background.

The main **Notifications** switch turns them all off at once.

## Not getting notifications?

- Check that the app is allowed to send notifications in the iOS Settings.
- Turn on **Background updates** in **Settings** › **Synchronization Interval**. The app watches for new grades and exam dates in the background.
- Subjects hidden in notifications don't send any (see **Filter and hidden subjects**).

### In the classic look

Set up notifications in **Menu** › **Settings** › **Notifications**.
