## What Today shows

The **Today** tab is part of the new look for University Plus subscribers. At the top you see the date and the week of the semester, and below it a large card with what's happening now: the current class with the time left, the next class, or the free time until it.

Below that you'll find:

- **Coming up**: the rest of today's classes.
- **Needs attention**: for example an expired sign-in or a notice from your university.
- **Exam sign-ups**: subjects with exam dates you can sign up for.
- **Upcoming exams**: the exam dates you're signed up for.
- **New grades**, **Offers**, **Friends** and **News** from your university.

Tap a class to open its details. Touch and hold a class to hide it or add a note. Pull down to refresh.

## Two classes at once

When two classes overlap, the card says **Classes at the same time**. Tap **I'm attending** on the one you're going to. The class above the tab bar, the widgets and the Live Activity follow your choice.

## Exam sign-up reminders

If a subject under **Exam sign-ups** shouldn't remind you any more, touch and hold it and choose **Don't remind me**. Bring it back in **Settings** › **Hidden exam sign-ups**.

## The class above the tab bar

Above the tab bar you see the current class, the free time until the next one, or the next class. Turn it off in **Settings** with **Class above the tab bar**.

### In the classic look

The classic look has no Today tab. You'll find the current and next class on the **Study** tab, and your favourite friends on the **Favorites** tab.
