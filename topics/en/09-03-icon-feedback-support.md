## App icon

In **Account** › **App icon**, choose one of the alternative icons.

## Feedback

In **Account** › **Feedback**, tell us about a bug, a sign-in problem or an idea for a new feature. If you'd like a reply, turn on **I want to receive reply to my feedback** and fill in your e-mail address.

## Supporting development

In **Account** › **Support development**, you can support the app's development with a small tip.

## About the app

**About app** has our contact details, the app version, the licences of the libraries used, and the terms and conditions.

### In the classic look

**Feedback**, **Tip jar**, **Help** and **About app** are in the **Menu**. Change the **App icon** in **Menu** › **Settings** › **Application**.
