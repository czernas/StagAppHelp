## iPhone and iPad

The app requires iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. On iPad, the tabs are in a sidebar.

## Older devices

An iPhone or iPad on iOS 15 to 17 keeps the last 2.x version.

## Mac with Apple silicon

You can also use the app on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). It runs the iPad version, which isn't fully adapted for the Mac, so some screens may behave unusually.
