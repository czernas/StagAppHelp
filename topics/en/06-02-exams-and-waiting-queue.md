## Exam and pre-exam dates

On the **Study** tab, **Exams** lists your subjects with exams and pre-exams. Tap a subject to expand its dates. When a subject has both, use **Show** to choose **All**, **Exams** or **Pre-exams**. Dates that are over are folded under **Past**.

![Exams](screenshots/study_exams_en.png)

## Signing in and out

Tap **Sign in** or **Sign out** on a date and confirm. You can also sign up from the **Today** tab, from an exam date in the timetable, or from the subject's page. Each date shows its occupancy and until when you can sign in and out.

## The waiting queue

When a date is full and STAG allows a queue, you'll see **Join queue**. As soon as a place frees up, you're signed in automatically. While you're queued, the date shows **In queue**. Leave the queue with **Leave queue**.

## Notifications about new dates

The app notifies you of a new exam date if you have that turned on (see **Notifications**).

### In the classic look

Exam dates are in your profile on the **Exams** tab (or **Menu** › **Exams**). Each subject has its own card. When a subject has both exams and pre-exams, switch between them with **Exams** / **Pre-exams**. **Sign in**, **Sign out**, **Join queue** and **Leave queue** work the same way.
