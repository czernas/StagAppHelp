## Offline

The app stores your timetable, grades, exam dates, subjects and study details on your phone, so you can see them without an internet connection.

## Data synchronization

The app refreshes its data at regular intervals, which you set in **Settings** under **Synchronization Interval**:

- **Timetable, Grades, Exams**: every 30 minutes by default.
- **Buildings, Rooms, Schedule, Friend's timetable**: every 12 hours by default.

**Background updates** let the app refresh data even when you're not using it. That's how notifications about new grades and exam dates reach you.

**Force data update** downloads everything right away. You can use it once every 15 minutes.

You can also pull down to refresh most screens (Today, Study, Campus, Friends, Rooms, Canteen…).

You'll find the settings in **Account** › **Settings**.

### In the classic look

You'll find the settings in **Menu** › **Settings**.

## When do I need the internet?

Whenever something has to be downloaded or sent right away, for example:

- signing in or out of an exam date,
- search,
- sharing your timetable with friends,
- offers and news from your university.
