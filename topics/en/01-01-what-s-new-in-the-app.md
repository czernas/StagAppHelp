### Version 1.7.7

**Sign in on VFU, UHK, JČU, OSU**

- The option to log in via OAuth at VFU, UHK, JČU, OSU has been added due to the blocking of the original login method by VFU, UHK, JČU, OSU.

### Version 1.7.6

**Sign in on ZČU**

### Version 1.7.5

**Sign in on TUL**

- The option to log in via OAuth at TUL has been added due to the blocking of the original login method by TUL.

### Version 1.7.4

**Notifications from university**

- Fixes app crash when displaying notification screen

### Version 1.7.3

**Alternative application icons**

Now you can choose one of several alternative app icons to make your app fit your phone's screen!

**Minor improvements**

- Fix application crashes

### Version 1.7.1

**Study**

The **Dashboard** screen is now called **Study** and contains the most important information about your studies, such as your current and next timetable activity, a list of the most recent credits and exams completed, and in the future a list of the next signed-in exams.

**Favorites**

On this screen you can now find all your favorite friends with their current and next timetable activity. In addition, you will also find opportunities to extend your study practice aimed directly at you with the job offers.

**Searching**

Search can now be found in the **Menu**.

### Version 1.6.0

**Friends**

The way of adding students to friends has been redesigned in the app. You can now only add friends via a QR code, making it safer for students and avoiding unwanted stalking.

**Feedback**

Feedback is now more intuitive - you have a choice of preset options to select from.

### Version 1.5.0

**Help**

There is the new **Help** tab in the **Menu** where you can find all important information about the application, how to use it and also a list of the most common problems and their solutions.

**Minor improvements**

- Added a switch to **Settings** to disable automatic search while typing.
