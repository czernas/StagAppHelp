## Version 3.0

**A new look for University Plus subscribers**

University Plus subscribers get a completely new look: the **Today**, **Timetable**, **Study** and **Campus** tabs, your account behind your initials at the top right, widgets, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and the current class above the tab bar. Without a subscription, the app keeps its classic look. Your data and settings are the same in both.

**For everyone**

- A new welcome tour on first launch
- New alternative app icons
- Help is downloaded from the web, so it's always up to date
- The app requires iOS and iPadOS 18 or later

### Version 2.7.0

- Signing in and out of exam dates and waiting queues works again. STAG had started rejecting these requests.
- Offers show up even on a device whose clock is set wrong.
- Newly downloaded offers appear without a manual refresh.
- A background data update no longer makes iOS close the app.

### Version 2.6.1

**Timetable**

- Switching to next week no longer keeps showing the current week in the week before a daylight saving change.
- This week and next week include Sunday, so weekend classes show again.
- For subjects that share a code across departments, the timetable shows the department too, and the detail opens the right subject.

### Version 2.6.0

- **Waiting queue:** join the queue for a full exam date. When a place frees up, you're signed in automatically.
- **Study progress:** a new profile tab with credits, subjects by semester and your thesis.
- **Current semester** with the end dates of classes and the exam period.
- **Timetable** shows the week number and its parity (odd / even) from the STAG calendar.
- **Canteens** for UPOL, JU and UTB, with outlet selection.
- Notifications about new grades and exam dates now say what's new.

### Version 2.5.1

- Your list of studies is refreshed from STAG regularly, and you can now refresh it yourself.
- Clearer error messages when signing in.
- Correct credit and exam counts, including S, Z, U and N marks, and no false new-grade notifications.
- Correct semester week numbers in the timetable.

### Version 1.7.1

**Study**

The **Dashboard** screen is now called **Study** and contains the most important information about your studies, such as your current and next timetable activity, a list of the most recent credits and exams completed, and in the future a list of the next signed-in exams.

**Favorites**

On this screen you can now find all your favorite friends with their current and next timetable activity. In addition, you will also find opportunities to extend your study practice aimed directly at you with the job offers.

**Searching**

Search can now be found in the **Menu**.

### Version 1.6.0

**Friends**

The way of adding students to friends has been redesigned in the app. You can now only add friends via a QR code, making it safer for students and avoiding unwanted stalking.

**Feedback**

Feedback is now more intuitive - you have a choice of preset options to select from.

### Version 1.5.0

**Help**

There is the new **Help** tab in the **Menu** where you can find all important information about the application, how to use it and also a list of the most common problems and their solutions.

**Minor improvements**

- Added a switch to **Settings** to disable automatic search while typing.
