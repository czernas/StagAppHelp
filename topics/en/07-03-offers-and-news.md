## Offers

The **Campus** tab has **Offers** for students. Tap one to open its details, and tap **I'm interested** to send the advertiser your contact details, and your CV if you like.

In **Settings** under **Offers**, you can turn on **I want relevant offers** and fill in an e-mail address the advertiser can contact you at.

## News

Below the offers is **News** from your university. Tap **All** to see everything.

## University notices

Notices your university sends you are in **Account** › **Notifications**. The most important ones also show on the **Today** tab under **Needs attention**.

### In the classic look

- **Offers** are in the **Menu**, on the **Study** tab and on the **Favorites** tab.
- **News** is on the **Explore** tab.
- **University notices** are in **Menu** › **Notifications**.
- Set **I want relevant offers** and the e-mail address in your profile, on the **Settings** tab.
