## Widgets

Widgets are for University Plus subscribers. Add them like any other widget: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap **Edit** › **Add Widget** and search for the app.

- **Now and next**: the class you're in, the next one, or the time until it. A small widget and a Lock Screen widget.
- **Today's classes**: the rest of today's classes, with the free time between them.
- **Exam dates**: your exam dates and the subjects still open for sign-up.

Widgets are updated each time the app syncs and download nothing themselves. If you don't open the app for a long time, they may show older data.

## Live Activity

When you open the app during a class or 15 minutes before one, a Live Activity with the current or next class appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

- If you swipe it away, it stays hidden for the rest of the day. Bring it back on the **Today** tab with **Show it again**, or in **Settings** › **Show the Live Activity again today**.
- Turn it off completely in **Settings** with **Live Activity**.

### In the classic look

The classic look has no widgets or Live Activity. Without a subscription, a widget says it comes with Plus.
