## The semester hasn't started yet

Before the semester starts, the timetable often doesn't show because:

- your classes are only pre-registered, and you aren't actually enrolled yet,
- STAG hasn't published your timetable yet. Wait until the semester starts at the latest.

## Check the dates and the filter

Make sure you've chosen the right **Dates** and that the timetable isn't hidden by the **Filter** or by hidden subjects (see **Working with the timetable** and **Filter and hidden subjects**).

## Still not showing?

- In **Settings**, tap **Force data update**.
- Change the app's **Language** in **Settings**, wait a moment and change it back.
- If your phone uses the 12-hour clock, switch to the 24-hour clock in the iOS Settings (**General** › **Date & Time**).
- Sign out and sign in again.

## Nothing helps?

Try deleting and reinstalling the app. If that doesn't help either, write to us through **Feedback** and include your username, university and an e-mail address we can reply to.

### In the classic look

You'll find **Settings** and **Feedback** in the **Menu**.
