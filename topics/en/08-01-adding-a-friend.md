## Adding a classmate with a QR code

Open Friends from the **Friends** tile on the **Campus** tab.

1. Your classmate taps **Share my timetable**. The app shows them a QR code that's valid for a short time.
2. You tap **Add friend via QR code** and scan it.

You'll then see their timetable in your app. Friends can only be added with a QR code, so nobody can follow your timetable without you knowing.

![Friends](screenshots/friends_en.png)

## A teacher's timetable

Tap **Add teacher's timetable** and search for the teacher.

## Who sees my timetable

**Sharing with** lists the people you share your timetable with. For each one, you can choose **Revoke access to my timetable**.

### In the classic look

Friends are in **Menu** › **Friends**, and **Share my timetable** and **Add friend via QR code** work the same way. Add a teacher with the **+** button, or with the star next to their name in search or on a subject. Under **Sharing with**, you can also pause sharing your timetable.
