## Filter

The funnel at the top left opens **Filter**. Under **Kind**, choose what you want to see: **Everything**, **Lectures**, **Practicals**, **Seminars**, or **Exams, credits and more**. While a filter is on, the funnel shows a dot.

![Filter](screenshots/timetable_filter_en.png)

## Hidden subjects

Subjects you don't need to see (for example ones already acknowledged) can be hidden in the timetable, in notifications, or in both.

- Touch and hold a class and choose **Hide in timetable**, **Hide in notifications**, or **Hide in timetable and notifications**.
- Manage all rules in **Account** › **Hidden subjects**, or in **Filter** with **Edit hidden subjects…**. There you can add a **New rule** and turn single rules off.
- The **Hide chosen subjects** switch in **Filter** shows hidden subjects again for a while.

### In the classic look

- Choose the activity type with the button at the top right, under **Activity type**.
- Manage the rules for hiding subjects in **Menu** › **Filters setup** (the button at the top left of the timetable). With **New filter**, pick a subject and a type and choose whether to hide it in the timetable, in notifications, or in both.
- To hide a subject quickly, use the **…** button in a class's details.
