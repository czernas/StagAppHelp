## List of all friends

In **Friends** tab in the **Menu** there you can view all friends together, including their current and first following timetable activities. From here it is also possible to click through to the details page, where you can find out more information about the friend and their timetable.

## Quick overview

Friends can be added to your favorites. A preview card with the current and next timetable action of a particular friend can then be found on the **Favorites** screen.
