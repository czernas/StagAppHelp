## Where to find the settings

Open **Account** behind your initials at the top right and tap **Settings**.

![Settings](screenshots/settings_en.png)

## What's in the settings

- **University Plus** (for subscribers): **Class above the tab bar** and **Live Activity**.
- **Synchronization Interval**: how often data is refreshed, **Background updates** and **Force data update** (see **How does the app work with data?**).
- **Notifications**: which notifications you want (see **Notifications**).
- **Hidden exam sign-ups**: sign-up reminders you hid on the Today tab.
- **Grades & timetable**: **Show study group timetable** and **Counting of the average**.
- **Offers**: **I want relevant offers** and an e-mail address for advertisers.
- **Application**: **App theme** (light, dark, by system), **Language**, **Autoscroll to current time in timetable** and **Automatic searching while typing search phrase**.

### In the classic look

The settings are in **Menu** › **Settings**: **Synchronization Interval**, **Notifications** and **Application** (icon, theme, language, tips, timetable autoscroll and searching while typing). **Show study group timetable**, **Counting of the average** and the offers settings are in your profile, on the **Settings** tab.
