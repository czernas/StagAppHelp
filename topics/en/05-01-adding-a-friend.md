## Finding a friend - Teacher

In the current version of the app, it is possible to add a friend - a teacher - and their timetable in the search or from a specific subject detail.

## Add a friend - Teacher

In the card with the teacher's name there is a star icon - click on it to add the teacher to your friends and see his/her schedule.

## Add a friend - Student

Go to **Menu** to the  **Friends** screen. On this screen, the classmate you want to add as a friend must click on the **Share my timetable** button. This will generate a QR code, which you will scan after clicking the **Add friend via QR code** button. Then the connection is made and you will see your friend's timetable in your app.
