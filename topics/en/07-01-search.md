## What you can search for

The **Search people, subjects, exams** field is at the top of the **Campus** tab. Choose what you're looking for: **People**, **Subjects** or **Exam dates**. The funnel button sets what to search by.

From the results, open a person's or subject's page, including their timetable, or sign up for an exam date.

## Searching as you type

To see results while you type, turn on **Automatic searching while typing search phrase** in **Settings** › **Application**.

Search needs an internet connection.

### In the classic look

Search is in **Menu** › **Search**. At the top, choose **Student**, **Employee**, **Subject** or **Exam**. When searching for a subject by name, use the language the app is set to.
