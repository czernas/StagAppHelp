## Display

The button at the top right (**Display**) switches between:

- **Day** and **Week**: swipe sideways to move between days and weeks.
- **List** and **Grid**: your timetable for a chosen range.

![Display options](screenshots/timetable_display_en.png)

## Dates

The calendar icon opens **Dates**. Choose **Today**, **This week** or **Next week**, and in List and Grid also **Next 30 classes**. In the calendar below, you can **Go to date** or pick a **Custom range**. You can also open the **Academic calendar** from here.

![Dates](screenshots/timetable_dates_en.png)

## Class details

Tap a class to open its details: when and where it is, the semester week, the floor, the teachers, **All dates** (with room changes) and **Subject details**. For an exam date, you'll see its occupancy and can sign up for it.

## Notes

In a class's details, or after touching and holding a class, choose **Add note**. A note can apply to **This date** or to **All dates** of the subject. Teachers' notes from STAG are marked **From IS/STAG**.

## Subject, teacher and room timetables

On a subject's, teacher's or room's page, the **Full timetable** card opens their timetable.

### In the classic look

- **Display:** the button at the top right opens a panel where you switch between **List** / **Table** and choose the activity type.
- **Dates:** tap the title at the top. Choose **Today**, **This week**, **Next week**, **Upcoming**, or a range in the calendar.
- **Week:** below the title you see whether it's an **Odd week** or an **Even week**, and the week number.
- **Notes:** in a class's details, tap **…** › **Note**.
