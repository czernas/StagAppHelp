## The room name is too long

Some rooms have a name too long to fit in a class in the timetable. You'll see the whole name in the class's details (tap the class).

## The room is missing from the details too

The data probably didn't download completely, for example because of a connection problem with STAG. In **Settings**, tap **Force data update**.

## Nothing helps?

Try deleting and reinstalling the app. If that doesn't help either, write to us through **Feedback** and include your username, university and an e-mail address we can reply to.
