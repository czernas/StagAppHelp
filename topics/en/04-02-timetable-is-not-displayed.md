## The semester hasn't started yet

We are often contacted by students before the start of the semester that their schedule is not showing up. Causes are:
- You have selected schedule activities for pre-enrolment, but the actual enrolment has not yet taken place
- STAG has not yet “flashed“ the schedule activities into the system - Wait until the beginning of the semester at the latest, when they should be flashed on the STAG side

## Date range selection

Make sure you have selected the correct date range for which you want to display the schedule (see **Help - Working with the timetable**).

## Timetable still not showing up?

- Tap the button **Force data update** in **Settings** tab in **Menu**
- Switch the language in **Settings** tab in **Menu**. Wait a moment and change the language back.
- Change the time format from 12-hour to 24-hour in the system settings.
- Sign out and sign back in.

## Previous solutions don't help?

Try uninstalling and reinstalling the application.

## Nothing helping?

Contact us via the feedback form in **Menu**, add your login name, university and an email to which we will contact you back for additional information.
