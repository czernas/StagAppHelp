## Rooms

On the **Campus** tab, open **Rooms**. With **Add rooms**, save the ones you care about and see right away whether they're **Free** or **Taken**. The **Free now** filter shows only free rooms. A room's page also has its **Full timetable**.

You'll only see rooms after signing in, as STAG doesn't show them otherwise.

## Canteen

If the app knows your school's menus, the **Campus** tab has a **Canteen** tile. Choose the day and the outlet. In **My allergy**, set the allergens the menu should warn you about.

## Academic calendar

The **Academic calendar** tile shows the academic year by month: classes, the exam period, holidays and more. You can also open it from **Dates** in the timetable.

### In the classic look

**Rooms**, **Schedule** and the **Canteen** are in the **Menu**. In Rooms, you can search by room name or browse the buildings.
