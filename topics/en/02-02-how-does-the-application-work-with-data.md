## Offline mode

The app allows you to view the most important information even if you don't have internet access.

## Data synchronization

Synchronization occurs at regular intervals according to the set values. In the default state, e.g. the timetable is updated every 30 minutes and e.g. building information every 12 hours. This setting can be changed in **Menu** in the **Settings** tab. Data synchronization can also be triggered manually (also in **Settings**).

![](sync_en.png)

## When is internet connection necessary?

These are situations where you need to synchronize data instantly, such as:

- Enrolling for an exam or credit
- Searching
