## Where to sign in

On the **Today** tab, choose your university and tap **Sign in**. You can also sign in from **Account** behind your initials at the top right.

### In the classic look

Choose your university and sign in on the **Study** tab, or at the top of the **Menu**.

## Signing in on your university's website

For most schools, tapping **Sign in** opens your university's sign-in page. Before that, the app shows you a screenshot of the part of the form to use. It's usually the school account sign-in, not the _IS/STAG user database_.

## Signing in with a username and password

For some schools (e.g. UPOL) you fill in **Username** and **Password** right in the app. Use the same credentials as for the web version of IS/STAG, not your e-mail.

## Conditions of use

Before signing in, turn on **I accept the conditions of use**.

## After you sign in

The app downloads your studies, timetable, grades and subjects. If you have more than one study, you'll find all of them in **Account** (see **Several studies and accounts**).
