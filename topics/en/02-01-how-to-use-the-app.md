## What is possible to view in the app?

The app contains the most important information you need to access during your studies, such as:
- Timetable
- Grades
- Enrolment for exams
- Empty rooms
- and much more…

In addition, the app also offers you the possibility to keep track of your friends' timetable or find a part-time job while studying.

Explore the full app and see what it has to offer! Find out more on the website <a href=\"https://www.univerzita.app\">Univerzita.app</a>

## Tips

In the app, you can enable the display of tips to guide you in using the app. They are usualy displayed on the screen where some additional explanation of the functionality is appropriate. You can also turn tips on (or off) in the **Menu** under the **Settings** tab.

## Onboarding

When you first started the application, you were shown an introductory tutorial where you could find all the basic information about the application. To view this tutorial again, press the **Sign out all users** button in the **Menu**.
