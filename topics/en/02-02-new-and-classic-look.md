## University Plus

University Plus subscribers get the app's new look and the features only it has:

- the **Today**, **Timetable**, **Study** and **Campus** tabs, with your account behind your initials at the top right,
- widgets on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen,
- a Live Activity with the current class on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island,
- the class above the tab bar.

Without a subscription, you use the classic look the app had in version 2.6.

## What stays the same

Both looks use the same data and settings. If you start or stop your subscription, you keep your hidden subjects, the timetable filter, your notes, notifications, signed-in accounts and exam sign-ups.

## Managing your subscription

Manage your subscription in **Account** with **Manage subscription**, which opens the subscriptions in the App Store.

This Help describes both looks. Where the classic look differs, you'll find it in the folded section **In the classic look**.
