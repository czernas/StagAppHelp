## Header

Header contains:
- Most important study information (number of credits, completed exams, etc.)
- Study year toggle to view study information
- Study switcher (e.g. if you have multiple branches or multiple studies)

## Subjects & grades

The first tab of the profile contains a list of subjects, sorted by year of study. You can also see the grades and credits earned in the subjects. Click on a subject to view additional information, such as the syllabus or the complete timetable.

## Exams and credits

The middle tab presents a list of available exam and credit dates. The individual subjects always have their own tab. When you click on the tab, you will see a toggle to switch between the credit or exam dates.

## Settings

The last tab contains settings where you can switch how the weighted average will be calculated.
